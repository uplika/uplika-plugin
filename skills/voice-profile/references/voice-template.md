# voice.md template

Adapted from Jakeschincariol/youtube-agent-skill `templates/voice.md` (MIT, license text in `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/youtube-agent-skill.MIT`). Not affiliated with the original author. Extended for every channel, not only YouTube.

Saved at `~/.uplika/<workspace-slug>/voice.md`, one file per workspace (brand). Every writing skill reads it.

```markdown
# Voice: <workspace or brand name>

## Who I am talking to
One person, described properly: who they are, what they already tried, what they want.

## Tone
Two or three words and one sentence on what that means here (for example: plain, warm, no hype).

## How I end posts
The closing habit: a question, a one-line summary, a call to action, nothing.

## Words I never use
The ones that are not mine ("unlock", "game-changer", "dive in", and so on).

## Words I do use
Phrases and habits that are mine. Keep them.

## What I will not claim
Numbers I cannot show, results that are not mine, tools I have not used.

## Signature
Hashtags, sign-off line, links that go at the end, if any. Per channel when they differ.

## Examples
Three to five of my own recent posts, pasted as they are (the best-performing ones if I know them).
```
